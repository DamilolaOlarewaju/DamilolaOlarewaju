## About Me

I'm **Dami** — a Computer Science student, developer, and coding educator. My interests sit at the intersection of **software engineering, AI, and education**.

I work on web and Android projects, experiment with AI-powered experiences, and teach young learners through **CodeWithDami Academy**. Teaching keeps me practising one of the most useful engineering skills: explaining a complicated idea clearly.

My long-term goal is to become an **AI engineer**. Right now, I'm building my foundation through practical projects, better documentation, testing, and learning how to take software from an idea to a usable experience.

**Founder & Coding Educator**

My approach is practical: help learners build something they can understand, explain, and improve.
